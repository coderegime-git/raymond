DEFINITIONS

I WOULD LIKE TO

Select the type of calculation you want to run. You can calculate either the maximum withdrawal amount for a period of time, or how long a specific withdrawal amount will last.

STARTING BALANCE

The initial total amount that you currently have invested. Include any balance of investment savings such as 401(k) plan, IRA, or non-qualified accounts that you wish to include in this analysis.

ANNUAL RETURN

The constant rate of return you expect from your investments, after taxes. The actual rate of return on your investments will vary depending on the types of investments you choose.

EXPECTED ANNUAL INFLATION RATE

The rate you expect for the average long-term inflation rate. A common measure of inflation in the U.S. is the Consumer Price Index (CPI).

AMOUNT OF DISTRIBUTION & YEARS OF DISTRIBUTIONS

The recurring sum withdrawn during each period, or the overall time frame over which you wish distributions to continue.

The educational tool and calculations provided are hypothetical examples and estimates only. Market volatility and investment returns fluctuate and are not guaranteed. Actual investment performance, taxes, and fees will affect the duration of withdrawals. Consult your financial professional.
