DEFINITIONS

ORIGINAL MORTGAGE AMOUNT

Original balance of your mortgage.

ORIGINAL MORTGAGE TERM

Total length, in years, of your original mortgage in years. Common terms are 15, 20 and 30 years.

ADDITIONAL PRINCIPAL PAYMENT

Extra principal payment you make. This amount will be used to further reduce your principal balance.

CURRENT MORTGAGE PAYMENT

Monthly principal and interest payment (PI) based on your original mortgage amount, term and interest rate.
