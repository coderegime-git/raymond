DEFINITIONS

CURRENT AGE
Your current age.
AGE AT RETIREMENT
Age at which you plan to retire. This calculator assumes that this year you will, for the first time, not make any contributions to your retirement savings. For example, if you retire at age 65, your last contribution would happen in the end of your 64th year.
ANNUAL HOUSEHOLD INCOME
Total annual earned pre-tax gross income for your entire household.
CURRENT RETIREMENT SAVINGS
Total amount that you currently have saved toward your retirement. Include all sources of retirement savings, such as 401(k)s, IRAs, and annuities.
RATE OF RETURN BEFORE RETIREMENT
The annual rate of return you expect from your investments until you reach retirement. This should reflect the aggregate return of all of your retirement savings accounts (401(k), traditional IRA, Roth IRA, taxable investments). Historical returns for the S&P 500 have averaged approximately 10%–11% before inflation since 1926.
RATE OF RETURN DURING RETIREMENT
The annual nominal rate of return you expect from your investments during retirement. Most retirees shift to a more conservative portfolio with greater bond exposure to reduce volatility and safeguard capital during distribution.
EXPECTED INFLATION RATE
The annual rate of inflation you anticipate over your planning horizon. Long-term average historical inflation in the United States has ranged between 2.5% and 3.5% annually.
YEARS IN RETIREMENT
The number of years you anticipate drawing income from your retirement assets. Planning for longevity (often 25 to 35 years) ensures your assets outlast your lifetime.
PRE-RETIREMENT INCOME NEEDED IN RETIREMENT
The percentage of your current pre-retirement household income you will require annually in retirement. Most financial planners recommend targeting between 70% and 85% of pre-retirement earnings to maintain your standard of living.
INCLUDE SOCIAL SECURITY BENEFITS
Check this box to include estimated Social Security retirement income in your calculation. Social Security is indexed to inflation and replaces a percentage of pre-retirement earnings based on average lifetime earnings.
