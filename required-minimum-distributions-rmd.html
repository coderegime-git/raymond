DEFINITIONS

REQUIRED MINIMUM DISTRIBUTION (RMD)

The minimum annual amount required by the IRS to be withdrawn from qualified retirement plans.

AMOUNT SUBJECT TO RMD

The prior year-end fair market value of all qualified retirement accounts subject to minimum distributions.

UNIFORM LIFETIME TABLE

The standard IRS actuarial table used to compute annual distribution divisors based on the account owner's age.
