DEFINITIONS

CURRENT AGE
Your current age.
AGE AT RETIREMENT
Age at which you plan to retire. This calculator assumes that this year you will, for the first time, not make any contributions to your retirement savings. For example, if you retire at age 65, your last contribution will happen at the end of your 64th year.
ANNUAL HOUSEHOLD INCOME
Total annual earned gross income for your entire household before taxes. Include all wages, salaries, bonuses, and professional compensation.
CURRENT RETIREMENT SAVINGS
Total assets currently set aside in retirement plans and accounts (401(k), 403(b), traditional IRA, Roth IRA, SEP, or taxable brokerage earmarked for retirement).
RATE OF RETURN BEFORE RETIREMENT
The annual rate of return you anticipate on your investments prior to retirement. The S&P 500 has averaged roughly 10%–11% annualized return since 1926 before inflation.
RATE OF RETURN DURING RETIREMENT
The annual nominal rate of return you anticipate during your retirement distribution phase. Portfolios often shift toward fixed-income and dividend-paying equities to minimize capital volatility.
PERCENT OF INCOME SAVED
The percentage of your annual pre-tax household income currently committed to ongoing savings and investment contributions each year.
EXPECTED SALARY INCREASE
Annual percentage increase you anticipate in your household income up until retirement.
YEARS IN RETIREMENT
The number of years you anticipate drawing retirement income. A typical retirement lifespan spans 20 to 35 years.
PRE-RETIREMENT INCOME NEEDED IN RETIREMENT
The proportion of your final working years' household income required to sustain your planned living expenses in retirement (standard benchmark is 70% to 85%).
EXPECTED RATE OF INFLATION
The projected annual inflation rate over your timeline. Historically, U.S. Consumer Price Index inflation has averaged approximately 3.0% annually.
MARITAL STATUS & SOCIAL SECURITY
Social Security benefits are estimated based on your income and lifetime earnings records. Checking married calculates primary plus spousal auxiliary benefits.
