DEFINITIONS

FEDERAL INCOME TAX RATE

Describes Filing Status and Federal Income Tax Rates on Taxable Income table to assist you in estimating your federal income tax.

MARGINAL TAX RATE

This is the percentage paid in federal taxes on additional income. To determine your marginal tax rate, this tool calculates your total federal income tax and then recalculates it assuming you receive additional income.

AVERAGE TAX RATE

This is your total federal taxes divided by your total income. The average tax rate is always lower, sometimes by a wide margin, than your bracket tax bracket or marginal tax rate.

STANDARD DEDUCTION

The standard deduction is used to reduce your taxable income if you do not choose to itemize deductions on Schedule A. For 2024, the standard deduction is $14,600 for Single filers and $29,200 for Married Filing Jointly.
