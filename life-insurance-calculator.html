DEFINITIONS

CURRENT LIFE INSURANCE COVERAGE

Total amount of life insurance coverage you currently have for yourself.

YEARS INSURANCE NEEDED

Number of years your spouse will need to use your insurance proceeds to provide for living expenses and child care.

INFLATION RATE

The expected annual inflation rate. The headline Consumer Price Index (CPI-U) was approximately 3.1% for the 12 months ending November 2023. Over the last 40 years the highest CPI occurred in 1980 at 13.5%.

RETURN ON INVESTMENTS

The annual rate of return for your investments. The actual rate of return is largely dependent on the types of investments you select. The Standard & Poor's 500 (S&P 500) for the 10 years ending December 31st 2023, had an annual compounded rate of return of 11.8%, including reinvestment of dividends.

INCOME TAX RATE

The percentage of your investment return that will be paid in taxes. Taxes are assumed to be payable annually at the end of the year.

CASH AND SAVINGS

Total you have at bank, checking accounts, savings accounts, or cash equivalents.

HOME EQUITY

Total amount of equity in your home that you are willing to use toward future expenses.

INVESTMENTS

Total value of all investments that you are willing to sell to meet your future expenses.

OTHER

Any other assets that you may be willing to sell or liquidate.

TAXES ON NON-INSURANCE SALES OF ASSETS

Taxes that are required to be paid on non-insurance assets.

FUNERAL COSTS

Expenses associated with a funeral.

PROBATE COSTS

Probate/court costs or estate administration fees.

UNINSURED MEDICAL COSTS

Any medical costs that are not covered by health insurance.

DEBT RETIREMENT

Credit card debt, auto loans, mortgages or other debt.

COLLEGE FUND FOR CHILDREN

Amount you wish to provide your surviving children for future college expenses.

OTHER EXPENSES

Any other debt that you would need to pay with your insurance proceeds.

SPOUSE ANNUAL EARNINGS

Annual earnings that your spouse earns.

SOCIAL SECURITY CHILD BENEFIT

Depending on your earnings, your children may qualify for Social Security benefits.

SOCIAL SECURITY SURVIVING SPOUSE BENEFIT

Benefits for your spouse based on Social Security entitlements.

OTHER INCOME

Any other income that your family will receive.

LIVING EXPENSES (WITH CHILDREN AT HOME)

Total monthly expenses with children living at home.

LIVING EXPENSES (AFTER CHILDREN LEAVE)

Total monthly expenses after your children have left home.

CHILDREN'S EDUCATION EXPENSES

Monthly expenses for your children's education.

RETRAINING AND EDUCATION FOR SPOUSE

Education expenses to cover costs of education or retraining for your spouse.

OTHER EXPENSES

Any other monthly expenses.
