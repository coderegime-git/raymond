STUDENT LOAN CONSOLIDATION AND DEBT PAYOFF

Loans > Calculator
Share

The student loan consolidation and debt payoff calculator applies two simple principles to paying off high-interest debt:

  1. Consolidate your existing student loans.
  2. Use your extra savings each month to pay off your highest interest debt sooner.

To apply this school of thought first consolidate to a lower interest rate on your student debt, then use the extra money you've saved toward your highest interest rate debt until paid off. Repeat this with each succeeding debt until you are completely debt free. You will be amazed at the time and money saved using this rollover method!

DEFINITIONS

TOTAL CURRENT STUDENT LOAN MONTHLY PAYMENT
Total payments for all of your outstanding student loans. If the current payment is less than the minimum payment for your new consolidated loan, there will be no payment savings.
TOTAL CURRENT LOAN BALANCE
Total amount of money you owe on all of your student loan debt.
TOTAL INTEREST FOR NON-STUDENT LOAN DEBT
The total cumulative interest charged on your other non-consolidated debt lines over the duration of repayment.
TERM IN YEARS
The term in years for your new consolidated student loan. A consolidation can extend to: 10 years for balance of less than $7,500; 12 years for balance of $7,500 to $9,999; 15 years for balance of $10,000 to $19,999; 20 years for balance of $20,000 to $39,999; 25 years for balance of $40,000 to $59,999; 30 years for balance of $60,000 or more. Any balance under $7,500 has a term of 10 years.
CONSOLIDATED STUDENT LOAN TERM
Term of your new consolidated monthly payment for your consolidated student loans.
MONTHLY SAVINGS
The difference between your current student debt payments and that of your new consolidated payment. If the current payment is less than the payment for your new consolidated loan, there will be no payment savings.
ADDITIONAL PAYMENT
An extra amount in addition to your monthly minimum payments that you will use to pay down your non-student debt each month as you work towards becoming debt-free. The higher this amount the faster your debt will be paid off. This amount should be equal to your monthly payment savings from consolidation, plus any additional money you have available or from paying down other debts that you can afford. This amount allows you to enter the amount higher or lower than your actual payment savings from consolidation. For the purposes of convenience and focus we suggest a minimum of $50/month, although you should use whatever you can truly afford, but you must stay disciplined if you are unable to meet your payment plan.
PAYMENT METHODOLOGY
Select a method from the options: "Pay off balance with the highest rate first" for quickest debt free results (debt avalanche). If no selection, payment method "Pay off balance with lowest balance first" (debt snowball).
MINIMUM PAYMENT METHODOLOGY
Method used to calculate minimum payments on credit cards. Most credit cards calculate minimum payment as 2% to 4% of your current balance, while our credit card minimum payment methodology calculates 2% of total outstanding balance as minimum payment. If no payment methodology is chosen, the system defaults to calculating 2% of the initial balance until paid off, which can increase the time to pay off your balance. A 2% default is used as minimum payment on balances over $1,000.
CREDIT CARD BALANCE
The total current balance for this credit card.
CREDIT CARD INTEREST RATE
The annual percentage rate you pay for the credit card. The rate you enter is used to calculate the interest on future credit card purchases. The length of time you have this credit card may be much longer than calculated if you make more purchases or if your APR is reset to a penalty rate.
CREDIT CARD PAYMENT
The actual current monthly payment. If you entered this item, with card minimum payment, then your credit card minimum will calculate as 1% of your current outstanding balance. With the 'use credit card minimum payments' box checked, your monthly payment will decrease as your balance is paid down. If unchecked, your current monthly payment will be kept the same, increasing payments to debt until your debt is paid off, which will shorten the time your balance is paid in full.
AUTO BALANCE
The total current balance on an installment auto loan.
AUTO INTEREST RATE
The annual percentage rate you pay on the loan. Does not spread interest rate to this loan. This calculator assumes you will make payments until the entire installment period is finished. The calculation assumes you make the specified monthly payments until the full balance is paid.
AUTO PAYMENT
The monthly payment. Enter the actual monthly payment for your auto. The loan is paid off when the balance is 0 to complete the remaining payments.
REMAINING LOAN PAYMENTS
The actual number of payments remaining on this debt. A balance on your current auto payment will decrease.
