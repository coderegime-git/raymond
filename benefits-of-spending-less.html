DEFINITIONS

MONTHLY SAVINGS

The total amount that you could save per month by spending less. This amount is calculated in each of our initial potential deduction budget areas.

ANNUAL RATE OF RETURN

The annual rate of return for your investment. This calculator assumes that your return is compounded monthly and your deposits are made monthly. The actual rate of return is largely dependent on the types of investments you select. The Standard & Poor's 500® (S&P 500®) for the 10 years ending December 31st 2025, had an annual compounded rate of return of 14.8%, including reinvestment of dividends. From January 1, 1970 to December 31st 2025, the average annual compounded rate of return for the S&P 500®, including reinvestment of dividends, was approximately 11.3% (source: www.spglobal.com). Since 1970, the highest 12-month return was 61% (June 1982 through June 1983). The lowest 12-month return was -43% (March 2008 to March 2009). Savings accounts at a financial institution pay less but carry significantly lower risk of loss of principal balances.

It is important to remember that these scenarios are hypothetical and that future rates of return can't be predicted with certainty and that investments that pay higher rates of return are generally subject to higher risk and volatility. The actual rate of return on investments can vary widely over time, especially for long-term investments. This includes the potential loss of principal on your investment. It is not possible to invest directly in an index and the compounded rate of return noted above does not reflect sales charges and other fees that investment funds and/or investment companies may charge.

YEARS TO SAVE

The total number of years you plan to save.

FEDERAL TAX RATE

The federal tax rate you expect to pay on your taxable investments.

STATE TAX RATE

The state tax rate you expect to pay on your taxable investments.

TOTAL ACCUMULATION BEFORE TAXES

Total value of your savings before taxes for taxable accounts. Tax-deferred accounts grow tax-free until withdrawal. However, if your savings is being invested into a taxable account, this has been account for and paid by you.

TOTAL ACCUMULATION AFTER TAXES

The total amount you would have accumulated in a taxable account, taking into account all federal and state taxes on your annual earnings.
