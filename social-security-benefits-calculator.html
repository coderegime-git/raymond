DEFINITIONS

FULL RETIREMENT AGE (FRA)
For anyone born in 1960 or later, Full Retirement Age is 67. Claiming before age 67 results in a permanent reduction of up to 30%, whereas delaying past FRA until age 70 yields an 8% per year delayed retirement credit (+24% maximum increase).
ANNUAL INCOME & AVERAGE INDEXED MONTHLY EARNINGS (AIME)
Social Security calculates your Primary Insurance Amount (PIA) based on your top 35 years of inflation-indexed earnings using statutory progressive bend points.
SPOUSAL BENEFIT
A non-working or lower-earning spouse is entitled to claim up to 50% of the primary earner's Full Retirement Age benefit, provided the spouse claims at their own Full Retirement Age.
