DEFINITIONS

ADDITIONAL EXPENSES WHEN SPOUSE WORKS
These are any additional expenses that you expect to incur when your spouse works. All of the sub-totals should be entered per pay period, such as childcare, vehicle fuel/insurance, professional wardrobe, and lunches out.
PAY PERIOD & GROSS PAY
Gross pay is your spouse's pre-tax salary per pay period before any taxes or voluntary deductions are withheld. The pay frequency establishes the annualized run-rate (e.g. 26 pay periods for bi-weekly).
401(k) / RETIREMENT PLAN CONTRIBUTION
The percentage of gross income contributed to an employer-sponsored qualified plan. These funds reduce current taxable income and build long-term wealth.
FICA PAYROLL TAXES (SOCIAL SECURITY & MEDICARE)
For 2024, Social Security tax is 6.2% on earnings up to $168,600. Medicare tax is 1.45% on all earnings with no statutory ceiling. Together, employee FICA taxes equal 7.65% of wages.
STATE AND LOCAL TAXES
Marginal state and local tax withholding. Because this is a secondary household income, it is often taxed at the household's top marginal tax bracket.
