DEFINITIONS

AGE OF CHILDREN

The current age of your children. The difference between this current age and the age they start college is the number of years you have to save.

AGE TO START COLLEGE

The age your child will begin college. The default is 18, but the list for child's age options spans up to 25.

ANNUAL TUITION

The current estimated costs for one year's tuition and fees. The amount should be per year, not total, and be specific to the school they may be interested in attending. The average published costs of college for the 2024-2025 school year (including tuition, room and board, books, supplies, transportation, and other personal expenses) as reported by The College Board:

College Type Tuition & Fees Additional Expenses Total Average Net Price (After Aid)
Public 4-Year (In-state resident) $11,610 $18,540 $30,150 $15,640
Public 4-Year (Out-of-state resident) $31,030 $18,540 $49,570 $31,540

*For the purpose of this calculator, all expenses are assumed to be due at the start of the year.

ROOM AND BOARD

The current estimated cost of one year's room, board, and any other expenses not included in annual tuition. This should include food, dial-ticket transit, campus utilities, transportation, and books and personal schedule, calculate the amount of months you might participate in the school they may be interested in attending. For the purpose of this calculator, all expenses are assumed to be due at the start of the school year.

EDUCATION COST INFLATION

This is the percentage that you expect educational costs to increase per year. Data provided by The College Board's "Trends in College Pricing 2023" report indicates and fees increased approximately 1.6% annually over the past 20 years. 2024 saw a smaller increase of approximately 4%.

PRESENT SAVINGS

The total amount you currently have saved for your children's education.

MONTHLY CONTRIBUTIONS

The dollar amount you plan to save per month toward your children's education. All amounts are assumed to be added to your account at the beginning of the month.

RATE OF RETURN

The dollar annually compounded rate of return you expect from your investments. This will dictate how effectively your portfolio returns will generate compounding gains to offset college costs.
