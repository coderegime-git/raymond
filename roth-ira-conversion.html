Now, any traditional IRA owner can convert all or part of their savings to a Roth IRA regardless of income. DEFINITIONS

EVALUATION NOTE
When converting a traditional IRA to a Roth IRA, you pay ordinary income tax on the amount converted in the tax year of the conversion. In exchange, all future earnings compound completely tax-free and qualified withdrawals are 100% tax-free.
AMOUNT TO CONVERT
The pre-tax balance in your Traditional IRA or qualified plan that you intend to convert to a Roth IRA.
TAXES PAID FROM OUTSIDE FUNDS
Paying conversion taxes using non-retirement taxable cash maximizes the amount compounding tax-free in the Roth IRA. If taxes are withheld directly from the converted amount, individuals under age 59½ may trigger a 10% IRS early withdrawal penalty on the withheld tax portion.
CURRENT TAX RATE
Your marginal tax rate today. Because a Roth conversion adds to your taxable income, large conversions may push part of your income into a higher tax bracket.
