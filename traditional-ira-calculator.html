DEFINITIONS

STARTING BALANCE
The current balance of your traditional IRA.
ANNUAL CONTRIBUTION
The amount you will contribute to your account each year. This calculation assumes that you make your contributions throughout each year, on an annual basis. The maximum allowed contribution for a traditional IRA is $7,000 or $8,000 if you are age 50 or older. This calculation adjusts for inflation in future years. The contribution phase-out limits only occur if your comprehensive modified adjusted gross income is above the IRS thresholds.
CURRENT AGE
Your current age. The account owner must be 18 or older for this calculation. Although it is possible to make contributions at any age provided you have earned income, this calculator uses standard adult parameters. Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs) must begin at age 73 (or 75 if you reach that milestone on or after 2033).
AGE AT RETIREMENT
Age at which you plan to retire. The age must be at least 59½ for this calculator to reflect full penalty-free distributions. While you can retire earlier, distributions before 59½ generally incur a 10% early withdrawal penalty in addition to regular income tax, with certain statutory exceptions.
MAXIMIZE CONTRIBUTIONS
When calculating future contributions, this will adjust whether you are able to adjust your contributions for inflation to make the maximum allowed contribution each year.
CATCH-UP CONTRIBUTIONS
Allows individuals aged 50 and older to contribute an additional $1,000 per year ($8,000 total for 2024).
NON-DEDUCTIBLE CONTRIBUTIONS
Portion of your traditional IRA contribution that was made without a tax deduction. Traditional IRA contributions are always made on a pre-tax basis unless your income exceeds workplace plan deduction phase-out limits. Non-deductible contributions still grow tax-deferred until retirement.

TOTAL TAX SAVINGS
Total cumulative tax reduction realized today from making tax-deductible contributions into your traditional IRA over your career.
EXPECTED RATE OF RETURN
The expected nominal rate of return on your traditional IRA investments. Past performance does not guarantee future results. Diversification does not assure a profit or protect against loss in declining markets.
CURRENT TAX RATE
Your current marginal income tax bracket used to calculate immediate tax deduction savings.
RETIREMENT TAX RATE
The expected marginal tax rate you expect to pay on your distributions at retirement.
