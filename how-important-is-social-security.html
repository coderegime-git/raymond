DEFINITIONS

SOCIAL SECURITY BENEFIT

Social Security is based on a sliding scale depending on your lifetime earnings, how long you work, and what age you retire. Social Security benefits automatically increase each year based on increases in the Consumer Price Index (COLA). In addition, spousal benefits allow a non-working or lower-earning spouse to receive up to 50% of the primary earner's benefit amount.

AGE AT RETIREMENT & CURRENT AGE

Age at which you retire defines your investment compounding timeline and full retirement age (FRA) entitlement under Social Security guidelines.

HOUSEHOLD ANNUAL INCOME & SALARY INCREASE

Your combined household earnings used to project future retirement living standards and baseline Social Security benefit tiers.

RATE OF RETURN & INFLATION

Assumed compound annual investment returns before and during retirement, net of advisory and administrative fees, offset by long-term cost of living inflation.

The educational tool and calculations provided are hypothetical examples and estimates only. Social Security rules and benefit projections are subject to future legislative reform. Consult a certified financial planner or the Social Security Administration (SSA.gov) for personalized statements.
