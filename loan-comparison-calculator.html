DEFINITIONS

LOAN AMOUNT

The total dollar amount for this loan.

INTEREST RATE

The stated interest rate for this loan.

TERM (YEARS)

The number of years over which you will pay off the loan. The most common terms are 1-5 years and 30 years. If the loan has a balloon payment, the term can be shorter than the amortization period.

AMORTIZATION

The number of years used in calculating the monthly payment.

ORIGINATION FEE

The dollar amount charged as a loan origination fee and is included in the annual percentage rate (APR) calculation.

CLOSING COSTS

Total of all closing costs for this loan.

ANNUAL PERCENTAGE RATE (APR)

A combined calculation of fees, interest and amortization used to compare different loan offers on an equal basis.
