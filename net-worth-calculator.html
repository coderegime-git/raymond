NET WORTH CALCULATOR

Personal Finance > Calculator

Your net worth is the value of all of your assets minus the total of all of your liabilities. Put another way, it is what you have minus what you owe. If this result is positive, then you have a surplus net worth. If you determine that you owe more than you have, then you have a negative net worth. This calculator helps you determine your net worth and estimates how it could grow or diminish over the next 10 years.
