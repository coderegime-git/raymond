DEFINITIONS

CURRENT MONTHLY EXPENSES

Your total monthly living expenses. Remember to include your home or rent payments, food, clothing, auto, phone and other monthly expenses.

DISABLED MONTHLY EXPENSES

Your monthly expenses while you are disabled. This amount is usually a little less than your regular monthly expenses. The default value for this field is estimated as 90% of your current monthly expenses. You should keep in mind, however, that many expenses such as your mortgage, rent, utilities and food will not likely reduce for someone before you are disabled.

MONTHS OF DISABILITY

The number of months you become disabled will prevent you from working. A common baseline is between 6 and 12 months.

CURRENT MONTHLY COVERAGE

Your current monthly disability coverage. Make sure to include any disability coverage provided by your employer.

MONTHS OF COVERAGE

Number of months that your current monthly coverage will last.

ANNUAL INFLATION

This is what you expect for the average long-term inflation rate. A common measure of inflation in the U.S. is the Consumer Price Index (CPI). From 1926 through 2023 the CPI has had a long-term average of 3.0% annually.
