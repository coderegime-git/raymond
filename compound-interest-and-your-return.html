DEFINITIONS

INVESTMENT AMOUNT

The amount of your initial investment.

INTEREST RATE

The rate of interest rate for your investment. The actual rate of return is largely dependent on the types of investments you select. The Standard & Poor's 500 (S&P 500) for the 10 years ended December 31st, 2023, had an annual compounded rate of return of 11.4%, including reinvestment of dividends. From January 1, 1970 to December 31st 2023, the average annual compound rate of return for the S&P 500, including reinvestment of dividends, was approximately 11.1 percent (with a standard deviation of 17.5%). The highest 10-year period return was 19.5% (June 1989 through June 1999). The lowest 10-year period return was -1.4% (March 1999 to March 2009). Savings accounts at financial institutions are not currently expected to return this level of annual return.

It is important to remember that these examples are hypothetical and that future rates of return cannot be predicted with certainty, and that investments that pay higher rates of return are generally subject to higher risk and volatility. The actual rate of return on investments can vary widely over time, especially for long-term investments. Also consider the potential loss of principal on your investment. It is not possible to invest directly in an index and the compounded rate of return quoted above does not reflect sales charges and other fees that investment funds and/or investment companies may charge.

YEARS

Number of years for the investment.

COMPOUND FREQUENCY

Interest on an investment's earnings, plus previous interest. The more frequently this occurs, the sooner your accumulated interest will generate additional interest. You should check with your financial institution to find out how often interest is being compounded on your particular investment.

YEARLY APY

Annual percentage yield received if your investment is compounded yearly.

QUARTERLY APY

Annual percentage yield received if your investment is compounded quarterly.

MONTHLY APY

Annual percentage yield received if your investment is compounded monthly.

DAILY APY

Annual percentage yield received if your investment is compounded daily.
