DEFINITIONS

CURRENT AGE
Your current age.
AGE AT RETIREMENT
Age at which you plan to retire. Contributions will be made continuously until retirement.
ANNUAL CONTRIBUTION
The amount you wish to contribute to a 401(k) each year. For 2024, the elective deferral limit is $23,000 for workers under age 50, with a $7,500 catch-up contribution permitted for those age 50 and older.
INVEST TRADITIONAL TAX SAVINGS
Because contributions to a traditional 401(k) are made pre-tax, they reduce your current income taxes. Checking this option assumes you invest those current tax savings annually into a taxable account at the same rate of return to create a fair side-by-side comparison with the Roth 401(k).
MAXIMIZE CONTRIBUTIONS
Automatically increase your contribution to the statutory elective deferral limit permitted by IRS tax law.
EXPECTED RATE OF RETURN
The anticipated annual nominal investment return of your portfolio. Historically, a diversified equity portfolio has yielded between 7% and 10% annualized long term.
CURRENT TAX RATE
The combined federal and state marginal income tax rate you pay on your highest dollar of earnings today.

RETIREMENT TAX RATE
The estimated combined marginal tax rate you expect to pay when you withdraw funds during retirement. Traditional 401(k) distributions are taxed as ordinary income, whereas qualified Roth 401(k) distributions are 100% tax-free.
AFTER-TAX TOTAL AT RETIREMENT
The net usable spending capital remaining after all federal and state taxes have been paid upon distribution at retirement age.
