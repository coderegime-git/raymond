DEFINITIONS

APPRAISED VALUE OF YOUR HOME

The current value of your home as determined by a recent appraisal, assessment, or automated valuation model.

OUTSTANDING HOME LOANS

Total amount of all outstanding loans and balances, including your first mortgage, second mortgage(s), and any other debt secured by your home.

LOAN-TO-VALUE (LTV) RATIO LIMIT

The maximum percentage of your home's appraised value that the lender will allow you to borrow against. For example, if the lender has an 80% limit and the home has an appraised value of $300,000, the maximum credit limit is $240,000 minus your $140,000 outstanding home loans, leaving you with an available credit line of $100,000.

The educational tool and calculations provided are hypothetical examples and estimates only. Credit approval and actual credit limits are subject to underwriting criteria, debt-to-income verification, credit score, and property appraisal requirements determined by the lender.
