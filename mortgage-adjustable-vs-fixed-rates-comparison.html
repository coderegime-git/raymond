DEFINITIONS

FIXED-RATE MORTGAGE

A fixed-rate mortgage has the same interest rate and monthly payment throughout the term of the mortgage.

FULLY AMORTIZING ARM

An adjustable rate mortgage where the initial rate is fixed for a specified period and then adjusts periodically. The monthly payment is calculated to pay off the entire mortgage balance over the term.

INTEREST ONLY ARM

An Interest-Only ARM requires monthly interest payments only during the initial fixed period, keeping initial payments low without paying down principal.

MORTGAGE AMOUNT

Original or expected balance for your mortgage.

INTEREST RATE CAP

The absolute maximum interest rate for the mortgage. The mortgage's interest rate will never exceed this interest rate cap.
