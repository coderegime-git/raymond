MUNICIPAL BOND TAX EQUIVALENT YIELD

Savings & Investments > Calculator

This calculator will estimate the tax-equivalent yield (TEY) for a municipal bond. Income generated from municipal bonds is not subject to federal income taxes and, in addition, if the bond was issued in your state of residence, it can also be free from state income taxes. Use this calculator to determine the yield required by a fully taxable bond to have the same after-tax return as a municipal bond.

DEFINITIONS

BOND YIELD

This is the annual yield of your municipal bond or bond fund.

ESTIMATED TAXABLE INCOME

Total income after exemptions, adjustments and deductions used to determine your marginal federal tax bracket.

TAX EQUIVALENT YIELD

The pre-tax yield that a taxable bond must earn to equal the tax-free return of a municipal bond: TEY = Muni Yield / (1 - Tax Rate).
