DEFINITIONS

APPRAISED HOME VALUE & TAXES
Current fair market valuation of your property along with annual county property taxes and hazard homeowners insurance.
ORIGINAL LOAN & PAYMENTS MADE
The initial principal borrowed and the total count of monthly mortgage payments fulfilled to date. This determines the outstanding mortgage balance remaining to be refinanced.
CLOSING COSTS & BREAK-EVEN POINT
Closing costs include lender origination fees, appraisal, title insurance, and escrow fees. The break-even horizon is the number of months of lower monthly payments needed to completely recover the upfront refinance closing costs.
