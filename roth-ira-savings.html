DEFINITIONS

STARTING BALANCE
The current balance of your Roth IRA.
ANNUAL CONTRIBUTION
The amount you will contribute to your Roth IRA each year. For 2024, the maximum annual contribution is $7,000 ($8,000 for individuals age 50 or older). High earners are subject to phase-out limitations based on Modified Adjusted Gross Income (MAGI).

CURRENT AGE & AGE AT RETIREMENT
Your current age and anticipated retirement age. Contributions are assumed to compound each year until retirement.
EXPECTED RATE OF RETURN
The expected average annual nominal rate of return for your investments in the account. Historically, the S&P 500 has averaged roughly 10%–11% before inflation.
MARGINAL TAX RATE
The combined federal and state income tax bracket that applies to your top tier of income. In a taxable investment account, this tax rate creates an annual tax drag on yields, dividends, and realized gains.
ROTH 5-YEAR RULE & QUALIFIED DISTRIBUTIONS
Withdrawals of earnings from a Roth IRA are 100% tax-free provided the account has been open for at least 5 tax years and you are at least age 59½, or meet other statutory exceptions (death, disability, first-time home purchase). Contributed principal can be withdrawn tax-free and penalty-free at any time.
