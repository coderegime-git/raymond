DEFINITIONS

RETIREMENT CONTRIBUTION %

This is the percentage of your gross annual salary you choose to save into your pre-tax retirement plan.

PAY FREQUENCY

How often you receive a paycheck (e.g. bi-weekly 26 pay periods per year).

TAX ADVANTAGE

Because contributions are pre-tax, every dollar contributed reduces your taxable income, softening the net impact on your paycheck.
