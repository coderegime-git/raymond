DEFINITIONS

INVESTMENT GOAL

The goal is the future value of your invested funds.

YEARS TO ACCUMULATE & INITIAL INVESTMENT

The number of years you have to save, and the starting capital you currently have invested.

PERIODIC CONTRIBUTIONS & FREQUENCY

The recurring dollar sum added to your account each period (monthly, quarterly, or annually).

RATE OF RETURN & INFLATION

Assumed constant compound annual yield earned on your portfolio, offset by federal and state income tax liabilities and long-term cost of living inflation.
