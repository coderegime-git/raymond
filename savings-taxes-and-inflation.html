DEFINITIONS

YEARS
The number of years you plan to save.
PERIODIC CONTRIBUTIONS
The amount you add each month to your savings. This calculator assumes monthly compounding of contributions and interest.
AMOUNT CURRENTLY INVESTED
Initial lump sum starting balance already set aside in your investment account.
EXPECTED RATE OF RETURN
The gross annual return expected before taxes and inflation. Over long horizons, equities have generated average historical compound returns between 8% and 11%.
FEDERAL TAX RATE & STATE TAX RATE
Your marginal tax rates. Taxes act as an annual drag on taxable investment yields and realized capital gains.
EXPECTED INFLATION RATE
The projected annual rate of consumer price inflation. Over the long run, inflation has averaged approximately 3.0% annually, reducing the future purchasing power of every dollar saved.
