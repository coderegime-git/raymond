### C. Kylie (Brand Identity & Color Contrast)
* **Feedback:**  
  > *"I overall think the website is very dark. The contrast is very high on all of the images which leads to my eyes straining trying to look at them. Also wish we had integrated more navy and white rather than the black and gold. Our main color in our logo is navy, so that should be more prominent."*
* **Core Issues Identified:**
  1. **Eye Strain & Darkness:** Heavy black backgrounds with high-contrast text and images are causing visual fatigue.
  2. **Brand Alignment:** The official Raymond James brand identity centers around **Navy Blue (`#00205B` / `#0C2340`)** and **White**, rather than an all-black and gold palette.
* **Status:** **PENDING CLIENT ALIGNMENT ⏳**

---

### D. Jeremy (Website Theme & Background)
* **Feedback:**  
  > *"Layout looks great but I don’t think the site should be dark. I think it would look cleaner, with a lighter (possibly white) background."*
* **Core Issues Identified:**
  1. Strongly endorses the layout structure and UX.
  2. Recommends shifting from dark mode to a **light/white clean background** to give the site a crisp, modern, corporate wealth management look.
* **Status:** **PENDING CLIENT ALIGNMENT ⏳**

---

### E. Pryia (Quality Assurance & Spell Check)
* **Feedback:**  
  > *"need to do spell check, one word that is misspelled is Counseling"*
* **Core Issues Identified:**
  * In US English corporate standards, *"Counseling"* has a single **'l'**. 
  * The navigation and file names currently use the British/Commonwealth spelling *"Counselling"* (e.g. `counseling-visionaries.html`, navigation dropdown labels).
* **Status:** **PENDING EXECUTION (Quick Win) ⏳**

---

### F. Curtis (Imagery, Tone, Usability & Features)
* **Feedback:**  
  > *"Overall, I think the site looks very professional and has a strong prestige and elegance to it. One recommendation I would make is to consider including a female athlete, such as a basketball or volleyball player, in the imagery. Given the darker color palette, the current design leans toward a more masculine aesthetic. I believe incorporating more balanced imagery and perhaps exploring a slightly different shade of gray could help create a more inclusive feel while still maintaining the sophisticated look and brand identity of the site.*  
  > *From a usability perspective, I found the website very easy to navigate. The transition between pages and tabs was seamless, and each section contained an appropriate amount of information without feeling overwhelming. I also particularly like the feature that displays current events and relevant market or world news directly on the site. I think this adds significant value for visitors. If possible, I would suggest updating this content periodically, similar to a news or sports ticker, to keep the website feeling fresh and engaging."*
* **Core Issues Identified:**
  1. **Gender Inclusivity:** The Athletes section currently features exclusively male athletes, which paired with dark styling feels overly masculine. Needs female athletic imagery (e.g., basketball or volleyball).
  2. **Palette Softening:** Suggests softer gray accents to balance the visual weight.
  3. **High Usability Praise:** Navigation and page transitions were rated seamless and intuitive.
  4. **News Ticker Feature:** Recommends turning the market/world news feature into an active/periodically updated news or sports ticker.
* **Status:** **PENDING EXECUTION ⏳**

1. **Navy & White Palette Implementation:**
   * Prepare a demonstration showing the primary Raymond James Navy (`#00205B`) with clean white backgrounds, replacing the heavy black backgrounds and gold accents.
   * Soften background grays to reduce eye strain.

### Phase 3: Interactive Polish
1. **Market/News Ticker:** Add a live or curated ticker bar at the top or bottom of key sections to keep market insights feeling fresh.
