DEFINITIONS

STARTING AMOUNT

The starting balance or current amount you have invested or saved. For this calculator, the tool assumes your current savings is earning your stated rate of return whether you decide to delay your new contributions or not.

ADDITIONAL CONTRIBUTIONS

The amount that you plan on adding to your savings or investment each period.

YEARS

The total number of years you plan on allowing to save or invest.

RATE OF RETURN

The annual rate of return for this investment or savings account. The Standard & Poor's 500 (S&P 500) had a long-term return of ~11.1% from 1970 to 2023.

YEARS TO POSTPONE SAVING

The number of years you might wait before you begin saving. This calculator will then delay your new contributions for this number of years.

FREQUENCY OF CONTRIBUTIONS

How often you make contributions to your account.

COST OF WAITING

The difference in your savings or investment balance between your delayed and non-delayed plans. All returns and future savings are before tax.
