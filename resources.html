Client Resource Guidance

Common questions regarding account access, publications, and strategic advisory coordination.

How do I access my monthly statements, tax 1099 forms, and performance reports?

You can securely view, download, and store your statements, trade confirmations, and tax documents anytime through Raymond James Client Access. If you need assistance setting up paperless delivery or linking accounts, our team is directly available at 215.860.7823.

Where can I find Raymond James' latest global economic forecasts and asset allocation updates?

Our flagship Investment Strategy Quarterly (ISQ) publication contains comprehensive analysis from Chief Investment Officer Larry Adam and the Investment Strategy Committee. We also provide personalized commentary during our regular client review meetings.

How does The Seiler Group coordinate with external CPAs, attorneys, and family offices?

We serve as the central hub of your advisory team, orchestrating seamless communication with your estate planning attorneys, certified public accountants, business managers, and sports agents to ensure all tax, legal, and investment maneuvers are completely aligned.

What specialized resources are available for newly drafted or traded professional athletes?

We offer dedicated onboarding roadmaps, multi-state tax forecasting, cash flow modeling for seasonal compensation, trust setup for asset protection, and financial education sessions designed to help athletes build enduring wealth far beyond their playing careers. Explore our Empowering Pro Athletes solutions.

Raymond James employs enterprise-grade 256-bit encryption, multi-factor authentication, continuous behavioral threat monitoring, and strict privacy protocols. Visit our Privacy & Security Commitment for complete details.
