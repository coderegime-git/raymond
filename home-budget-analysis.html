DEFINITIONS

GROSS SALARY / PAY

Your total gross pay received from your employment before any payroll deductions or taxes.

OTHER INCOME

Any other regular cash inflows received, including business dividends, alimony, child support, pensions, or rental profits.

FEDERAL TAX WITHHOLDING

Total amount withheld for federal income taxes. Enter this amount directly from your pay stub.

STATE TAX WITHHOLDING

Total amount withheld for state taxes. Enter this amount from your pay stub.

LOCAL TAX WITHHOLDING

Total amount withheld for city or county local taxes. Enter this amount from your pay stub.

OTHER TAXES AND WITHHOLDING

Total amount withheld for any other state disability or non-federal withholding taxes on your pay stub.

FICA

Total withholding for Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA / Social Security) from your salary.

MEDICARE

Total withholding for Medicare based on the gross income reported on your pay stub.

INSURANCE AND BENEFITS

Total amount withheld for medical, dental, life, and vision benefits offered by your employer.

COMPANY RETIREMENT / 401(K) PLAN

Total amount deducted from your paycheck and contributed to a qualifying retirement plan such as a 401(k), 403(b), or 457.

MORTGAGE AND RENT

Monthly housing costs including your primary mortgage principal and interest, rent, property taxes, homeowners insurance, and association dues.

UTILITIES

Monthly recurring domestic utility bills such as electricity, heating fuel, gas, water, municipal sewer, garbage pickup, mobile phones, cable TV, and broadband internet.

FOOD AND GENERAL EXPENSES

Day-to-day living costs including household groceries, restaurant dining, personal apparel, gasoline, vehicle transit, and discretionary entertainment.

INSURANCE

Out-of-pocket insurance policies not deducted through payroll, including personal automobile insurance, private term life policies, umbrella liability, and individual healthcare premiums.

MAINTENANCE, MEDICAL, CHILDCARE, ETC.

Recurring family obligations such as property repairs, automotive service, childcare, daycare tuition, medical co-pays, prescription medication, and minimum credit card debt obligations.
