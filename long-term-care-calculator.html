Long-term care is needed by those who can't perform the basic tasks required to take care of themselves. This can include people suffering from a debilitating illness or a chronic injury. The need for long-term care can arise unexpectedly, often resulting in a significant financial burden. This calculator helps you determine if you are financially prepared for this impending possibility.

DEFINITIONS

ESTIMATED DAILY COST

The expected current estimated cost of long-term care. This is your expected estimated cost of long-term care. According to the Genworth Financial 2023 Cost of Care Survey, the national median cost of care for a semi-private room was $104,000 per year for a semi-private room. This is about $285 per day.

YEARS OF CARE

The number of years you expect to require long-term care. The average stay in a nursing home is approximately 2.5 years, but can vary considerably.

INFLATION RATE

The rate at which you expect the cost of healthcare to increase each year. Long-term care costs typically increase faster than general consumer inflation.

WHAT DOES THIS CALCULATOR DO?

Provides estimates of the potential cost of long-term care to assist in your financial and insurance planning.
