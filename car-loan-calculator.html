DEFINITIONS

CALCULATION FOR

Choose what you would like to calculate. You can choose to calculate the payment or the purchase price.

MONTHLY PAYMENT

The amount you pay each month for your auto financing.

TOTAL PURCHASE PRICE (BEFORE TAX)

This is the total cost of your auto purchase. Include the cost of the vehicle, additional options and destination charges. This amount does not include sales tax. Enter an initial estimation for you, not included on your total purchase price.

TERM IN MONTHS

Number of months the loan will be in effect.

INTEREST RATE

Annual interest rate for this auto loan.

REBATE AND CASH DOWN

Total amount of cash and/or rebate rebate applied to the purchase. The larger your cash down payment the smaller the loan that you will need to finance the purchase.

TRADE-IN ALLOWANCE

The total amount that you are given for any car/vehicle that you trade in on your new purchase. A trade-in allowance can helps reduce the amount of taxes you pay in state. See the definition for "No sales tax deduction for trade-in" for more information on how a trade-in affects auto taxes.

AMOUNT OWED ON TRADE

Total sum balance still outstanding on the trade-in.

REGISTRATION FEE (OPTIONAL)

Any additional fee that is not subject to sales tax. This usually includes the license fees or any other fees that may be payable on delivery and registration.

OTHER FEES (OPTIONAL)

Any additional fee that is subject to sales tax. This is usually doc fees (doc fees/prep fees or any other fees that may be part of delivery and sale price).

SALES TAX RATE

Sales tax percentage rate charged on the purchase.

NO SALES TAX DEDUCTION FOR TRADE-IN

If you live in a state where your trade-in tax allowance does not reduce your total purchase, check this box. In the following states, state tax is calculated on the purchase price, less trade-in allowance: Alaska, California, District of Columbia, Hawaii, Maryland, Michigan and Montana. Also note that if you live in a state without sales tax, or if you purchase a vehicle in a state other than where you live, you will pay taxes based on laws of the state where you title and register the car.
