DEFINITIONS

AMOUNT OF WITHDRAWAL
The amount you wish to withdraw from your qualified retirement plan. Most withdrawals are subject to ordinary income taxes in the year of distribution.
CURRENT AGE
Your current age. If you are under age 59½, you may be subject to a 10% federal early withdrawal penalty on the taxable amount distributed, unless a specific statutory exemption applies (e.g., permanent disability, qualified medical expenses, substantially equal periodic payments).
FEDERAL INCOME TAX RATE
Your marginal federal income tax bracket. If you are unsure, the calculator will default to 22%. Please review the 2024 Federal Tax Bracket table below to assist you in determining your marginal rate.
