HOW UNCERTAINTY AROUND AI REGULATION COULD AFFECT MARKETS

09.28.26 // Economy & Policy // Video // Share
NEXT ARTICLE

Raymond James Washington Policy Analyst Ed Mills discusses how uncertainty around artificial intelligence regulation could affect markets, dissecting legislative proposals, antitrust enforcement, and policy implications for technology infrastructure.

Key Regulatory Dynamics Analyzed

DYNAMIC 01

Federal vs. State Legislative Patchwork

As individual states introduce bespoke AI safety and disclosure standards, the lack of a cohesive federal framework presents compliance complexities for multi-state technology operators.

DYNAMIC 02

Antitrust & Market Concentration

Scrutiny from federal trade and justice agencies regarding exclusive partnerships between foundational model architects and cloud hyperscalers continues to evolve.

DYNAMIC 03

National Security & Export Controls

Bipartisan consensus remains firm around semiconductor fabrication access, compute clusters, and international trade restrictions on advanced silicon hardware.

DYNAMIC 04

Investment Horizons & CapEx Resilience

Despite regulatory headwinds, hyperscaler capital expenditure programs in sovereign data centers, power utilities, and enterprise AI tools remain foundational.
