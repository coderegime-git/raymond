TAX DEDUCTIBILITY VS TAX-FREE DISTRIBUTIONS
Traditional IRA contributions may be tax-deductible now, reducing your current tax liability, but distributions in retirement are taxed as ordinary income. Roth IRA contributions are made after-tax, but both principal and earnings are withdrawn 100% tax-free in retirement.
