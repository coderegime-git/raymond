DEFINITIONS

ORIGINAL INVESTMENT

The amount of your investment before suffering your losses. This is the goal you are seeking to recover.

CURRENT VALUE

Your current remaining balance after your losses.

ADDITIONAL CONTRIBUTIONS

The amount you will contribute to help accelerate your wealth restoration.

EXPECTED RATE OF RETURN

The annual rate of return you anticipate earning from your diversified recovery portfolio.
