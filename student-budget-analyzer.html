DEFINITIONS

PAYMENT TIMING OPTIONS
Each expense or income item can be budgeted by academic year, semester, or month.
Standard Higher Education Academic Schedule
Schedule Frequency Description
Academic YearOne annual lump-sum allocation covering the complete 9 to 12 month school year.
Semester 1 & 2Two periodic disbursements (typically August/September for Fall, January for Spring).
MonthlyRecurring monthly expenditures (rent, groceries, phone, subscriptions).
SCHOOL EXPENSES
Tuition, university registration fees, course books, lab materials, software licensing, and academic fees.
LIVING & HOUSING EXPENSES
On-campus residence hall fees, off-campus apartment rent, electricity, water, high-speed internet, and renter's insurance.
FINANCIAL AID & CONTRIBUTIONS
Scholarships, Pell Grants, institutional aid, subsidized/unsubsidized federal student loans, family support, and on-campus work-study earnings.
